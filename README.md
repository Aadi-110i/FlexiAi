# Orbit

Orbit is an infinite workspace where you can chat with AI, search the web, take notes, and write code -- all on one limitless screen. Use it to arrange your tools side-by-side, visualize how your ideas connect, and stay completely focused. It is the simplest way to turn messy thoughts into clear, organized work.

## The Problem Solved

Modern workflows are fragmented. We constantly switch between browser tabs for AI chats, web searches, documentation, and code editors. This context-switching breaks focus and scatters information across disconnected windows. 

Orbit solves this by giving you a single, infinite spatial canvas. Everything you need is laid out exactly where you want it, visible at a glance.

## Why You Should Use It

- Stop juggling tabs: Bring all your essential tools into one unified view.
- Maintain context: Keep your research, code, and notes side-by-side. 
- Visualize connections: Spatially arrange your work to map out complex projects.
- Stay focused: A distraction-free, minimalist environment designed for deep work.

## Core Features

- Infinite Canvas: Pan and zoom across a limitless workspace.
- AI Chat Containers: Communicate with AI models in resizable windows.
- Code Editor: Write, format, and execute code snippets directly on the board.
- Web Search: Pull live information without ever leaving your workspace.
- Notes and Drawing: Jot down ideas or sketch out diagrams seamlessly.

## Cool Stuff Under the Hood

- Native Fullscreen: Individual containers can "break out" of the canvas and fill your entire screen for focused deep-work.
- Persistent Storage: Your layout, positions, and container sizes are automatically saved locally. You never lose your setup.
- Minimap Radar: A dynamic radar system helps you navigate your sprawling workspaces and locate distant containers instantly.
- Glassmorphism UI: A premium, dark-mode aesthetic with amber accents, built for visual comfort during long sessions.
